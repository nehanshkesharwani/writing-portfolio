A little about the work

I like writing that finds the person inside the subject. Whether I am writing about someone's academic or professional experience, exploring an idea, or simply telling a story, I am usually looking for the part that makes it feel real and worth reading.

My writing moves across forms and subjects. I write interviews, narrative pieces, articles, blogs, and stories, and I am just as comfortable with nonfiction as I am with fiction. I enjoy adapting my voice to what a piece needs rather than fitting every idea into the same kind of writing.

A lot of my work begins with curiosity. Sometimes that means a conversation with someone, sometimes research, and sometimes just an idea I cannot stop thinking about. From there, I work on the quieter parts of writing too: what to keep, what to leave unsaid, how to structure a piece, and how it should feel when someone reads it.
